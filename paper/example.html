A Shift, resolving

X opens with a Magnet, so O must answer beside it. O plays a Stinky, so X must go somewhere not beside that — and X's Shift lands bottom left and names up. The column slides, and what falls off the top comes back at the bottom.

Where the duel is fought

The arena, small enough to read at a glance. A space a side has taken carries that side's mark — a plain X or O, and nothing else; the veto and the space's name sit along the top of it.
