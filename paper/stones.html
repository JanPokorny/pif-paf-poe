The stones

Three move things, one refuses to be moved, two say where the opponent may play. The dashed square follows the stone just played.

Shift

Name a direction: that row or column slides one step, and what falls off the end wraps to the other.

2048

Name a direction: every stone on the board slides that way as far as it goes.

Rotate

Pick one of the 2x2 blocks it belongs to: those four squares turn one step clockwise.

Mountain

Nothing moves it, and nothing slides past it. Here someone else's 2048 falls around it.

Magnet

Your opponent must play next to it. Rings show where.

Stinky

Your opponent must play away from it. Only the latest of these two binds.
